{{/* A page's types, lower-cased and deduped: its top-level section ("page" at root), each of `collections:` (a value or a list), and "bookmarks" when it carries a bookmark URL (bookmark-urls.html). The only reader of `collections:`; `type:` is left to Hugo (layout choice) and adds nothing. Feeds the type chips, the Pagefind `type` filter, index.json `types`, and type-pages.html — so a top-level section lists every page of its type, wherever it's filed. Expects a page. */}} {{ $types := slice (or .Section "page") }} {{ with .Params.collections }} {{ range (cond (reflect.IsSlice .) .
