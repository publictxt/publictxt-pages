# Curated Commons: Open Aggregation for the Open Web

_A PublicTxt Project Post_

---

There's a quiet irony at the heart of the modern web. We have Creative Commons licensing for content, but the *conversation* around that content — the commentary, the curation — still belongs to the platform.

**Curated Commons** is an attempt to fix that: Creative Commons for the curation layer. #commons #publictxt #writing

## The problem with centralised aggregation

Most aggregation platforms extract value from a single point of control. You can't fork a conversation or subscribe to a curator across sources.

## What changes

- Multiple communities can maintain separate curated threads on the same source material.
- Curation becomes a publishable, citable artifact.
- Communities can fork curated collections.

[[PublicTxt]]'s community features build reputation from transparent contribution history stored in Git.
