# bliki

A **bliki** is a blog + wiki hybrid: dated posts alongside evolving topic pages, all in one repository. #wiki #blog

The term was popularised by Martin Fowler. In a PublicTxt repo the `blog/` and `wiki/` folders play the two roles, and pages link freely across them — e.g. this post: [Curated Commons](../blog/20260509-CuratedCommons-Open-Aggregation-for-the-Open-Web.md).
