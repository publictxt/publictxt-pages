[#plaintext](/tags/plaintext/) [#writing](/tags/writing/) [#publictxt](/tags/publictxt/)

Notes stay Markdown files in their repo, readable and editable anywhere. What the site needs — titles, dates, tags — is derived at build time, into a copy; the source is never rewritten.
